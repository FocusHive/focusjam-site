Privacy Policy

  • Home
  • Privacy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how FocusJam ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit focusjam.com (the "Site") or use the FocusJam meeting service and its AI Jammer (the "Service"). It forms part of our Terms of Service.

When a business customer uses the Service, that customer decides how its meetings are used, and we process meeting content on its behalf and on its instructions. For our own Site, accounts and billing, we decide how personal information is used.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, organisation, and the role or seat your administrator assigns you. Guests who join through an invitation give a display name.
  • Meeting media: the audio and video of a meeting. It is end-to-end encrypted between participants' devices and is not recorded or stored by us. Meeting recording is currently not offered.
  • Transcripts and AI outputs: when the AI Jammer is enabled for a meeting, the speech it hears is transcribed, and the transcript, notes, decisions, action items and recaps it produces are stored encrypted in your organisation's workspace.
  • Service and security data: technical information needed to run and protect the Service, such as IP address, browser type, connection quality, meeting and room identifiers, timestamps and security logs.
  • Sales and support: what you send us when you request a demo, email us, or buy a plan, such as your name, work email, company, team size and message, and billing details for invoices.

3. How we use information

  • to provide the Service: host meetings, run the AI Jammer, and show you your transcripts and recaps;
  • to secure the Service: authenticate users, block abuse and automated attacks, and investigate incidents;
  • to support you, answer enquiries, and send service and billing messages;
  • to bill team and organisation customers;
  • to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, we do not use meeting content or AI outputs to train AI models, and we do not show advertising.

4. AI processing

Speech recognition, speech synthesis and language-model processing run on infrastructure operated for FocusJam. Meeting content is not sent to third-party AI providers. AI outputs can contain errors, and you can review, correct or delete them.

5. Sharing

We share personal information only:

  • with service providers that help us operate the Site and Service, such as website hosting, content delivery and edge security, email, and payment processing, under contracts that limit their use of it to providing their service to us;
  • within your organisation, as your administrator configures (for example, meeting recaps shared with participants);
  • when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the Service;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this Policy;
  • with your consent.

6. Security

We protect information with end-to-end encryption for meeting media, encryption of stored transcripts and AI outputs, single sign-on with role-based access, private networking for administrative systems, and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected customers of a personal-data breach as the law requires. See our security page for more.

7. Retention

We keep account information while your account is active. Transcripts and AI outputs are kept until you or your organisation delete them, or for the shorter period your plan or administrator sets. Deleted content can be recovered for 30 days and is then permanently removed. Live in-meeting state is held only temporarily and expires within 7 days. We keep billing records for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict some processing. To use these rights, email hello@focusjam.com. If your account belongs to an organisation, we may refer your request to its administrator. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Cookies

The Site does not use advertising or analytics cookies or third-party trackers. The Service uses only the cookies and local storage needed to sign you in, keep your session secure, and remember your device settings.

10. International transfers

We operate the Service from the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your information is processed in the United States, and we protect it as this Policy describes.

11. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date and, for material changes, notify account administrators before the change applies.

13. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: hello@focusjam.com. Support: support@focusjam.com.
